# spec-v877 — Ishoo angioedema staging

## What this gives you

The stage, taken from the most distal site swollen, with the three things the number is not.

## §1 Four stages, by site

| Stage | Site | In the original series |
|---|---|---|
| I | Face or lip | Managed on a ward or discharged |
| II | Soft palate | Managed on a ward or discharged |
| III | Tongue | Need for intensive care admission and airway intervention rose sharply |
| IV | Larynx | Airway intervention most often required |

## §2 The stage is a site, not a severity score

This is why the tile exists. Nothing is added up: a large facial swelling is still stage I, and a
modest tongue swelling is stage III. When more than one site is recorded, the tile says which one
the stage came from and that the others do not raise it. On every result.

## §3 It describes disposition risk, not airway patency right now

A patient at any stage whose airway is threatened is an airway emergency, and the staging does not
defer that. The tile takes "the airway is threatened" as an input and says plainly that it
overrides the stage.

## §4 Stridor and voice change are late

Their absence does not make a stage III or IV swelling safe, and progression over hours is the
expected course of bradykinin-mediated angioedema. Printed at stage III and above.

## §5 It does not separate the mechanism

Bradykinin-mediated angioedema — the angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor and hereditary forms
— does not respond to epinephrine, antihistamines or corticosteroids, and the site of swelling
does not tell you which mechanism this is. On every result.

## §6 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- Ishoo E, Shah UK, Grillone GA, Stram JR, Fuleihan NS. *Predicting airway risk in angioedema:
  staging system based on presentation.* Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg. 1999;121(3):263-268.

No tracked guideline issuer, so no `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It stages a finding already examined. It does not decide the
airway, and it does not choose a drug.
