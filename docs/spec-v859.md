# spec-v859 — Priapism Cavernous Blood Gas Classification

## What this gives you

Which of the two priapisms this is, from the measurement that actually separates them — and the
clock, which is the part that is usually lost.

## §1 The gas

Blood drawn from the corpus cavernosum, not from an arm.

| | pO2 | pCO2 | pH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ischemic | under 30 mmHg | over 60 mmHg | under 7.25 |
| Non-ischemic | over 90 mmHg | under 40 mmHg | about 7.40 |

A gas that matches neither set is settled by color duplex: absent or minimal cavernosal arterial
flow is ischemic, normal or high flow is non-ischemic. A gas that is decisive outranks the
duplex, because it is the more direct measurement of the thing being asked about.

## §2 Two opposite emergencies

This is why the tile exists. **Ischemic** priapism is a compartment syndrome of the erectile
tissue: closed, hypoxic, acidotic, and decompressed rather than observed. **Non-ischemic**
priapism is an unregulated arterial inflow — not ischemic, usually not painful, and not an
emergency. Aspiration and an intracavernosal sympathomimetic are the treatment for the first
and treat the wrong disease in the second.

Anything the tile cannot resolve is returned as **handled as ischemic until it is shown not to
be**, because that is the type where waiting costs tissue.

## §3 A trauma history does not classify the episode

Perineal or straddle trauma is the classic story for the non-ischemic type. It is also the
classic reason an ischemic episode gets watched instead of decompressed. When the history is
entered and the measurements do not read as non-ischemic, the tile says so in those words.

## §4 The duration is the prognosis

| Hours | |
|---|---|
| Under 4 | Inside the window before it becomes a compartment syndrome |
| 4 to 24 | A compartment syndrome; necrosis of the cavernous smooth muscle begins around 24 |
| 24 to 36 | Past the point where that necrosis begins |
| Past 36 | Erectile function rarely preserved; intervention is for pain and detumescence |

Stated on every ischemic and every undetermined result. Left blank, the tile asks for it rather
than passing over it.

## §5 Sickle cell disease does not change the acute treatment

Hydration, oxygen, analgesia and exchange transfusion are systemic care. The guideline is
explicit that they must not delay or replace the local intervention.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It classifies an episode from measurements already taken. It
does not choose a drug, a dose, a shunt, or an embolization, and it is not a substitute for
urgent urological assessment.
