# spec-v1164 — the house says it a fourth way

[The programme map](incomplete-input-program.md) recorded that
`probe-omitted-field-decides`'s first section was 13 rows and every one had been
read. Re-run after this session's waves, it printed **15**.

That is the map's own point — *"a probe whose every row has been read is not a probe
with nothing left to say: the catalog moves and the rows change with it"* — so the
question was which rows were new, and whether any of them was mine.

## Two were `elapss`, and they were not a regression

```
elapss|el-loc
  omitted -> ELAPSS 16 to 21 of 40: the location is not stated, and it is worth 5 points.
  but el-loc=pcomPost -> ELAPSS 21/40: growth risk ~25.8% at 3 years.
```

The reading is exactly right — [spec-v1141](spec-v1141.md)'s disclosure, doing its
job. `abnormal` is `total >= 20`, so supplying the location crosses it, and the probe
flags any such crossing **unless the reading asks or discloses**.

It discloses. `ASKING` and `DISCLOSING` just did not know the words.

## `not stated`

`DISCLOSING` knows *"not entered"*, *"was not entered"*, *"not assessed"*. It did not
know **`not stated`** — which is what the two aneurysm scores say, and what
`impede-vte` and `glim-malnutrition` say:

| phrase | library files | occurrences | on the list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `not entered` | 46 | 63 | yes |
| `not stated` | **10** | **15** | **no** |

The same drift as `can only add` in [spec-v1094](spec-v1094.md) and `is needed` in
[spec-v1097](spec-v1097.md): one rule, two hands, one phrasing known.

**Measured before adding**, as that list's own rule requires. Across every tile and
every field it moves **four** rows from flagged to exempt — three on `impede-vte`
(*"IMPEDE VTE between 5 and 9 on what was entered: the thromboprophylaxis … not
stated"*) and one on `glim-malnutrition` (*"Not yet assessable by GLIM: the weight
loss and the body mass index … not stated"*). All four are tiles disclosing
correctly, and none of them answers anyway.

First section: **15 → 12.**

## The one genuinely new row, read

`rosendaal-ttr`'s two target-INR bounds, new since [spec-v1155](spec-v1155.md) made
them optional. Change the low bound from 2.0 to 3.0 and the time in therapeutic range
falls from 80% to 0%.

That is not a gap being filled — **the target range is a prescription, not an
observation.** Changing it asks a different question, and the reading names the range
it used every time: *"16 of 20 days in range 2–3"*. It stands, and the map's table
records why.

## The second section, re-read while the first was open

[spec-v1103](spec-v1103.md) read it at 13 rows; it is 34 now, and **every one is a
tile behaving correctly**. Ten are `oswestry-odi`, whose every label says *"omit if
not applicable"* and which is scored as a percentage of the sections completed. Four
are `pk-suite`, which **drops** the derived quantities needing a missing input rather
than computing them from zero — no volume of distribution, no half-life or loading
dose, and the maintenance dose still shown. `iol-power` names its assumption in the
verdict: *"**Emmetropic** IOL power 20.05 D"*. The map's table records the rest.

The third section has grown from the 32 rows [spec-v1104](spec-v1104.md) read to 58,
and is the standing next read. It is the weakest of the four by construction — the
band moves but `abnormal` does not — and spec-v1104 is the wave that found
`phoenix-sepsis` in it, so weak is not the same as empty.

## And four rows left without anyone touching them

`kings-college` and the three ICHD-3 headache tiles are no longer in the section. The
catalog moved under the probe between two readings of it, which is the reason the
table in the map is dated rather than permanent.
