# spec-v868 — CDC/CSTE pertussis case definition

## What this gives you

The surveillance classification — confirmed, probable, or not met — with the two readings of it
that cost a real case stated on the result itself.

## §1 Clinical criteria

In the absence of a more likely diagnosis, a cough illness lasting at least **two weeks** with at
least one of:

Paroxysms of coughing · Inspiratory whoop · Post-tussive vomiting · Apnea, with or without
cyanosis (**infants under one year only**)

## §2 Case classification

| | |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | An acute cough illness of any duration with a positive culture; **or** the clinical criteria with a positive PCR; **or** the clinical criteria with an epidemiologic link to a laboratory-confirmed case |
| **Probable** | The clinical criteria met, with neither laboratory confirmation nor an epidemiologic link |

## §3 It is a surveillance definition, not a treatment threshold

This is why the tile exists. Treatment and post-exposure prophylaxis are decided on clinical
suspicion and do not wait for a case to be classified. The sentence prints on every result.

## §4 A negative test does not move a case out of probable

Culture and PCR yield falls once the cough has run three to four weeks or antibiotics have
started, and the definition is built to classify those cases on clinical grounds instead. This
prints on every result that is not confirmed.

## §5 Serology is in no branch of it

Stated on every result, because it is the test most often sent and the one the definition never
uses.

## §6 Apnea counts only under one year

Ticked for an older child it is a real finding that this definition does not accept, and the tile
says exactly that rather than silently dropping it. The infant read also names why the two-week
cough is the wrong shape for a young infant, which is why the culture branch takes a cough
illness of any duration.

## §7 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- Council of State and Territorial Epidemiologists / CDC. *Pertussis (Whooping Cough)
  (Bordetella pertussis) 2020 Case Definition.* National Notifiable Diseases Surveillance System.

CDC is a tracked issuer, so a `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed and added.

## §8 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It applies a published surveillance definition to findings
already recorded. It does not diagnose pertussis, and it does not decide whether to treat.
