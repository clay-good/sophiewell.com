# spec-v895 — Gadolinium and NSF risk

## What this gives you

What the agent group and the kidney state together say about nephrogenic systemic fibrosis — and
which of the two is actually doing the work.

## §1 The grouping

| Group | Agents | |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Gadodiamide, gadopentetate, gadoversetamide | Associated with the greatest number of NSF cases |
| **II** | Gadobenate, gadobutrol, gadoterate, gadoteridol, gadopiclenol | Few or no unconfounded cases; risk possibly not distinguishable from zero, **even below an eGFR of 30 or on dialysis** |
| **III** | Gadoxetate | Limited data |

## §2 The agent group matters more than the eGFR

This is why the tile exists. The blanket rule that gadolinium is contraindicated below an eGFR of
30 came from **Group I** experience and has not applied to Group II agents for years — yet it is
still applied to them. On every result. When the agent is not entered the tile says so first,
because that is the input the answer turns on.

## §3 Dialysis after the scan is not a preventive measure

It is not required for a Group II agent, and starting dialysis solely to remove contrast has
never been shown to prevent the disease. A patient already on a schedule is dialysed on that
schedule. On every result.

## §4 Acute kidney injury is its own category

Not stable chronic disease at the same number. A filtration rate computed during an acute injury
does not describe a steady state, so the eGFR alone understates the uncertainty. Printed when
acute injury is the state entered.

## §5 The eGFR is not the only question about gadolinium

Retention, pregnancy and prior reactions to a contrast agent are separate matters this tile does
not address, and it says so rather than reading as a general clearance.

## §6 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- American College of Radiology. *ACR Manual on Contrast Media*, section on nephrogenic systemic
  fibrosis and gadolinium-based contrast agents.

The ACR is not in the tracked-issuer pattern, so no `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It applies a published grouping to an agent and a filtration
rate already known. It does not choose an agent, and it does not authorize a scan.
