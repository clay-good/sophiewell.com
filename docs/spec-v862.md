# spec-v862 — M-CHAT-R/F Toddler Autism Screen

## What this gives you

The total, and what the instrument actually says to do with it — which for the middle band is
neither of the two things people do.

## §1 The bands

Twenty items, for children between 16 and 30 months.

| Total | | |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Low risk | No further action on this screen |
| 3 to 7 | Medium risk | **Administer the Follow-Up.** On it, 2 or more is a positive screen |
| 8 to 20 | High risk | **Bypass the Follow-Up** and refer now |

A positive screen means referral for a diagnostic evaluation and for an early intervention
eligibility evaluation.

## §2 A medium score is neither a referral nor a pass

This is why the tile exists. A total of 3 is an instruction to administer the Follow-Up.
Referring every child who scores 3 over-refers heavily; discharging them misses the children the
instrument was built to find. The Follow-Up is what makes the screen work, and the tile returns
no verdict at all in that band until a Follow-Up score is entered.

At 8 or more the Follow-Up is bypassed. Enter one there anyway and the tile says it is not used.

## §3 Three items are reverse-scored

On items 2, 5 and 12 the at-risk answer is yes; on the other seventeen it is no. Scoring all
twenty the same way is the arithmetic error. The rule prints on every result, along with which
of the three scored on it.

## §4 A negative screen is not an exclusion

Screening is not diagnosis, and surveillance continues at every visit. A child screened before
the second birthday is screened again after it, and the tile says so when the age is under 24
months and the total is low.

Ages outside 16 to 30 months are scored, and told that the total does not carry its published
meaning there. Unanswered items are named and counted rather than scored as anything.

## §5 Item wording is not reproduced

Each item is named by its topic. Scoring is positional and never derived from text, so a relabel
cannot change a total. This follows the house rule already applied to fourteen other
instruments.

## §6 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- Robins DL, Casagrande K, Barton M, Chen CA, Dumont-Mathieu T, Fein D. *Validation of the
  Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised with Follow-up (M-CHAT-R/F).* Pediatrics.
  2014;133(1):37-45.

No tracked guideline issuer, so no `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It scores a screening instrument. It does not diagnose autism,
and no total it produces replaces a diagnostic evaluation.
