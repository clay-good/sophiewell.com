# spec-v830 — Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Level and Genotype

## What this gives you

Enter a serum alpha-1 antitrypsin level, a genotype, or the reasons for testing; get the
interpretation — including the fact that the threshold most people reach for does not do the
job they reach for it with.

## §1 The numbers

**Deficiency** is a serum level below **100 mg/dL** (20 µmol/L). The classical **protective
threshold** is **57 mg/dL** (11 µmol/L).

## §2 The protective threshold has been refuted, and that is why this tile exists

The 11 µmol/L figure was used for decades to predict who would develop COPD. The evidence now
shows that is inaccurate: **no data demonstrate a COPD risk that turns on that threshold**,
and genotype — PI\*ZZ and rare ZZ-equivalents — is the indicator independently associated with
disease.

A tool reporting "above the protective threshold, therefore not at risk" repeats a specific,
published error. So this tile states the refutation **in both directions** whenever a level
is entered:

- a level **above** the threshold is **not reassurance**;
- a level **below** it is **not a risk estimate**.

And when no genotype has been determined, it names that as the missing piece — because
genotype, not the level, is what the evidence ties to disease. `PI*MZ` is reported as a
carrier state with a modest smoking-amplified risk and explicitly **not equivalent to ZZ**.

## §3 The units are deliberately not interconverted

The published pairs are 57 mg/dL with 11 µmol/L, and 100 mg/dL with 20 µmol/L. Those imply
**different conversion factors** — 5.18 and 5.0 — because they are conventional paired values
rather than one exact conversion.

Converting between them would invent a precision the literature does not have. So each unit
is compared against its own published thresholds and neither is derived from the other. A
test asserts that the same *number* means different things in the two scales, which is the
whole point.

The result also carries the laboratory caveat: nephelometry and radial immunodiffusion do not
return the same value for the same sample.

## §4 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- ATS/ERS statement: standards for the diagnosis and management of individuals with alpha-1
  antitrypsin deficiency. *Am J Respir Crit Care Med.* 2003;168(7):818-900 — the definition,
  the two thresholds, and the testing indications.
- Franciosi AN, Hobbs BD, McElvaney NG, Carroll TP. Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency:
  clarifying the role of the putative protective threshold. *Eur Respir J.*
  2022;59(2):2101410 — the refutation.

ATS is a tracked issuer. The staleness row records that this tile deliberately reports the
2022 refutation rather than the 2003 statement's original claim.

## §5 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It interprets results already obtained. It does not order
testing or start augmentation therapy.
