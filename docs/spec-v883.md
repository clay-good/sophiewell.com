# spec-v883 — Fukuoka guidelines (branch-duct IPMN)

## What this gives you

Which of the two consensus tiers a pancreatic cyst falls in — and, more to the point, the fact
that the two tiers carry different recommendations.

## §1 The two tiers

**High-risk stigmata** — any one is a reason to consider resection:

- Obstructive jaundice attributable to a cystic lesion of the pancreatic head
- Enhancing mural nodule **≥ 5 mm**
- Main pancreatic duct **≥ 10 mm**

**Worrisome features** — any one calls for endoscopic ultrasound:

Pancreatitis · cyst ≥ 3 cm · enhancing mural nodule < 5 mm · thickened or enhancing cyst walls ·
main duct 5–9 mm · abrupt caliber change with distal atrophy · lymphadenopathy · raised CA 19-9 ·
growth ≥ 5 mm over two years

## §2 A worrisome feature means endoscopic ultrasound, not resection

This is why the tile exists. The two tiers are routinely conflated, and the whole design of the
guideline is that the middle tier buys more information rather than an operation. On every
result.

## §3 A cyst ≥ 3 cm is a worrisome feature, not a high-risk stigma

The 2017 revision moved size down from where the 2012 version had it, so size alone does not
carry a case to resection. Printed whenever size is the finding and no stigma is present.

## §4 The same measurement lands in either tier

A main duct **≥ 10 mm** is high-risk and **5–9 mm** is worrisome. An enhancing mural nodule
**≥ 5 mm** is high-risk and **< 5 mm** is worrisome. The number decides the tier — and the nodule
must be **enhancing** in both. On every result.

## §5 The jaundice must be attributable to the cyst

Jaundice from another cause is not a stigma, and that attribution is a clinical judgment this
tile cannot make. Printed whenever jaundice is recorded.

When both tiers are populated, the tile says the worrisome features do not add to the high-risk
tier — any one stigma reaches it alone.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It sorts findings already recorded into published tiers. It does
not decide whether to operate.
