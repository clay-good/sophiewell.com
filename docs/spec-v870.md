# spec-v870 — Neuroleptic malignant syndrome diagnostic criteria

## What this gives you

The consensus priority total, with the thing the number is not — a measure of how sick the
patient is — stated on the result itself.

## §1 Eight weighted findings, summing to 100

| Points | Finding |
|---:|---|
| 20 | Dopamine antagonist exposure, or dopamine agonist withdrawal, within the past 72 hours |
| 18 | Hyperthermia above 100.4 °F on at least two occasions, measured orally |
| 17 | Rigidity |
| 13 | Mental status alteration |
| 10 | Creatine kinase at least four times the upper limit of normal |
| 10 | Sympathetic nervous system lability |
| 7 | Negative work-up for infectious, toxic, metabolic and neurologic causes |
| 5 | Hypermetabolism |

**74 or more** supports the diagnosis.

## §2 It is a diagnostic-priority scale, not a severity scale

This is why the tile exists. The Delphi points weigh how much each finding argues **for the
diagnosis**. A higher total does not mean a sicker patient and does not grade anything. The
sentence prints on every result.

## §3 Neither fever nor rigidity is required

The threshold is reachable without hyperthermia (82 points remain) and without rigidity (83).
This matters because presentations associated with atypical antipsychotics may lack one or both,
and a total assembled without them is the one most likely to be dismissed. Printed whenever
either is absent.

## §4 The negative work-up is a scored item, not a precondition

It contributes 7 points and does not gate the total. The diagnosis stays one of exclusion in
practice, so when it is not recorded the tile says which causes it stands in for.

## §5 Below the threshold is not an exclusion

A total under 74 says the case would not be counted by these criteria, not that the syndrome is
absent. The tile prints the shortfall and says so.

Two more read-backs: exposure is the single largest item and dopamine **agonist withdrawal**
counts as exposure, which is the branch most often missed; and the creatine kinase item asks for
four times the upper limit of normal, not any elevation.

## §6 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- Gurrera RJ, Caroff SN, Cohen A, et al. *An international consensus study of neuroleptic
  malignant syndrome diagnostic criteria using the Delphi method.* J Clin Psychiatry.
  2011;72(9):1222-1228.

No tracked guideline issuer, so no `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It applies published criteria to findings already recorded. It
does not diagnose the syndrome, and it does not decide whether to stop a drug or start treatment.
