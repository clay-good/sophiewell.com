# spec-v834 — ESHRE Criteria (Premature Ovarian Insufficiency)

## What this gives you

Enter age, menstrual history and FSH; get whether the 2024 ESHRE criteria are met — and, when
they are not, which of the several ways the answer can be obscured is in play.

## §1 The algorithm

**Under 40 years of age:**

- **Bilateral oophorectomy = the diagnosis.** The guideline states no further testing is
  needed.
- Otherwise: **≥4 months** of amenorrhea or oligomenorrhea, **with an FSH >25 IU/L**.

The oophorectomy question therefore sits **first in the form**, before any laboratory field.
Putting it after the FSH would imply a test that cannot change the answer.

## §2 Four things the guideline says that tools drop

**FSH needs no cycle-day timing.** The guideline says so explicitly. It is a common and costly
misconception that it must be drawn on day 2–5.

**Hormonal therapy cuts both ways.** It can *conceal* the menstrual disturbance or cause
amenorrhea itself, **and** it lowers FSH — a combined oral contraceptive should be stopped for
**2–6 weeks** before the FSH is measured. A normal FSH drawn on the pill is not reassurance,
and the tile says so when exactly that combination appears.

**Estradiol is not diagnostic.** A low level adds confirmation; the diagnosis does not rest on
it.

**Ultrasound is not required, and AMH should not be the primary diagnostic test.** A small
ovarian volume or low antral follicle count is supportive only.

## §3 One point where the guideline versions differ

The **2016** ESHRE guideline asked for a raised FSH on **two occasions at least four weeks
apart**. The **2024** algorithm states the diagnosis on a raised FSH with the menstrual
criterion and does not repeat that requirement.

Rather than silently pick one, the tile reports the diagnosis on the current algorithm **and**
notes whether a confirmatory second sample was taken — because ovarian activity fluctuates and
FSH can fall back into the normal range, which makes the repeat clinically meaningful under
either wording. The note is suppressed once a repeat is recorded, and on the oophorectomy
route, which involves no FSH at all.

## §4 Sourcing (spec-v97 gate)

- ESHRE Guideline Group on POI. Evidence-based guideline: premature ovarian insufficiency.
  *Hum Reprod Open.* 2024;2024(4):hoae065 — the algorithm, the oophorectomy rule, the
  cycle-timing point and the hormonal-therapy caveat were taken from the guideline's own
  healthcare-professional toolkit.

## §5 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It applies published criteria to results already obtained.
It does not start hormone replacement or arrange fertility counseling.
