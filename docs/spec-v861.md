# spec-v861 — Spinal Epidural Abscess Decision Guideline

## What this gives you

Who gets imaged, before a deficit makes the decision for you.

## §1 The pathway

For a patient who already has spine pain.

| | |
|---|---|
| Any neurologic deficit | Image now |
| A risk factor, no deficit | Sedimentation rate; over 20 mm per hour, image |
| No risk factor, no deficit | Not selected for imaging on this pathway |

The five risk factors: injection drug use, an indwelling vascular access device, a site of
infection elsewhere in the body, immunosuppression (including diabetes, HIV, cancer, dialysis
and alcohol use disorder), and a spinal procedure or spinal surgery within the past year. Any
one opens the pathway.

## §2 The classic triad is the trap

This is why the tile exists, and it is stated on every result. Fever, spine pain and a
neurologic deficit together appear in only a small minority of confirmed cases. Waiting for the
triad is the delay, and the delay is what turns a treatable infection into a permanent deficit.

## §3 Fever is absent in about half

It is not an entry criterion here. Whenever a risk factor is present and no fever was entered,
the tile says that its absence removes nothing.

## §4 The sedimentation rate is the test, not the white count

The white cell count is normal in a large share of confirmed cases. It is accepted only so the
tile can refuse it as reassurance: enter a normal one and the tile says so in those words.

## §5 Neither negative result is an exclusion

"No risk factor" and "rate at or below 20" both stop the pathway rather than the concern. The
guideline was built to decide who gets imaged. A patient whose risk factor was never asked about
never enters it at all, and the tile says that too.

- Davis DP, Salazar A, Chan TC, Vilke GM. *Prospective evaluation of a clinical decision
  guideline to diagnose spinal epidural abscess in patients who present to the emergency
  department with spine pain.* J Neurosurg Spine. 2011;14(6):765-770.

No tracked guideline issuer, so no `docs/citation-staleness.md` row is owed.

## §7 Posture

Decision support, not a verdict. It selects patients for imaging. It does not diagnose or
exclude a spinal epidural abscess, and no pathway through it replaces the judgment of the
clinician at the bedside.
